# The Garden of Dreams Walk-through

## Destiny, Dreaming.

To start the main quest;
- Sleep. You will be directed by a dream to find the Morag Tong.	
- Speak to any Morag Tong member.			(You may enter the questline here directly without the dream.)
- Choose ‘The Garden’ topic and agree to the meeting.
- You will be directed to find the Monastery of Mephala on Azura’s coast. If you are not a member of the Morag Tong they will be hostile.
-  Sleep in the bed of the marked room. 			(You can also enter here)
- Talk with the Duchess.
- The golden ‘key’ to open the boat hold is deeper in the cavern.
- Enter the boat, then exit it again.
- Speak with Misfortune.
- Speak with the Duchess.
- Enter the White Cliffs through the portal on the northern wall of the Atrium.
- A spirit will direct you to the Tower, to unlock it you need to find the spirits watching over four beacons, complete the task they give you, then return to them. The tasks are;
	- Slay a loud beast further up the hill from the spirit.
	- Receive a shrine blessing from one of the nine walls and return with it to the spirit
	- Bring the spirit samples of a local flower.
	- The spirit is cold. Set them on fire.
		- Note; there are several spirits guarding doors that are tied to sidequests. They do not count 			toward opening the Tower.
- The Tower will open when all four spirits are satisfied and the beacons are extinguished.
- Stand on the button in the centre of the room.
- Enjoy the scenery.
- There is a sealed door to the north, you need to find celestine deposits and activate the blood marked braziers in the middle of the tower.
- Enter the corpsefield and navigate your way to the bottom.
- Confront Misfortune.
	- The fight has three phases. Damage Misfortune until they are forced to retreat.
- When Misfortune is defeated exit the area through the portal back to the Atrium.
- Receive a reward from the Duchess.


## Side Quests

### Lost Footsteps

- A spirit is waiting by the entrance to the Shifting Void in the White Cliffs. Talk to them and agree to help. They want you to find the exit for them.
- You must explore thoroughly for the exit to open.
- The flames at the final door will turn blue when you approach, return to the spirit for your reward.

## The Morag Tong

### Grandmaster Summons

 - At the rank of Brother or higher ask for a writ at any master, you will be directed to speak with Eno Hlaalu.
	- You may also ask Eno Hlaalu about writs directly at the appropriate rank.
- Eno will send you to the Monastery of Mephala on Azura’s Coast.
- Speak with the Master of the Subtle Kill in the refectory to complete the quest and be put immediately into the next one.

### The Monastery of Mephala

- The Master of the Subtle Kill wishes for you to make introductions to the other Masters and your fellow students.
- speak to all of them.
	- Either of the twins will do, they are very codependent.
		- The Master of Conquered Doubt will hand you a ledger to pass along.
- Return to the Master when introduction are done to receive your writ.

### Writ for Raynil	_+	A Study in Subtlety

The quest and writ are tied together.

- Ask about your ‘Writ’.
- You will first need to assign the students to writs. You already have the ledger of kills outstanding, read the first set and use your best judgement who to send to which job.
	- Ask each student about ‘Writs’ and give them the one you think works best.
		- Return to the Master of the Subtle Kill to receive your writ.

- Raynil is in Caldera, Shenk’s Shovel. You will be given a poison and strongly suggested you find a way to use it as a demonstration of your skill in subtlety. This is optional but strongly encouraged.
	- If you talk to Raynil’s mercenaries one will suggest giving him a Mazte to improve his mood.
	- the ‘Mazte’ topic will give you the option to slip him the poison. It takes several days to take effect.
- Return to the Master to be rewarded.
- The students will have returned too, and you will be told how each of them did. If you assigned them all perfectly you will be given a Morag Tong uniform as a bonus.
	- If somehow they all failed your leadership potential will be put into question and the questline ends.
- You are directed to The Master of Overt Violence for the next writ.

### Writ for Shepherd	+	The Pursuit of Violent Teachings

The quest and writ are tied together.

- Ask about your ‘Writ’.
- You will again need to assign the students to writs. Read the second set and use your best judgement who to send to which job.
	- Ask each student about ‘Writs’ and give them the one you think works best.
		- Return to the Master of Overt violence to receive your writ.

- Shepherd is in Gnisis. The Master suggests you could eliminate the rest of Shepherds gang but it is not a strict requirement. The people of the town will know more about their location.
	- Shepherd is across the river from Gnisis in a small camp. His accomplices are across the river from 	the eastern exit to Gnisis, and up on the hill to the west of Gnisis.
- Kill Shepherd, and optionally his accomplices.
- Return to the Master to be rewarded. If you killed them all you will receive a Daedric Knife.
- The students will have returned and again you will be told how each of them did. If you assigned them all perfectly you will be given a Morag Tong uniform as a bonus.
	- If somehow they all failed the questline ends.
- You are directed to The Master of Conquered Doubt for the final writ and test.

	- you may instead read the letter, and choose if you want to talk to the Masters about it instead of 	delivering it.

### An Eighteen Drake Run of Bad Luck

- After making introductions during ‘The Monastery of Mephala’ talk to either sibling, they want you to pick up a dead drop near Sadrith Mora. 
- go to the beach south west of Sadrith Mora, 19,3. Look for a small iron box marked by a red lantern.
- when you open the box you will be ambushed.
	-if you kill the ambusher you will get slightly different dialogue later and anenchanted ring off their 	body.
	- if you open the letter a new path opens to report its contents to the Master of Conquered Doubt.

- Take the letter to whoever you believe should have it.
	-siblings give gold and glass throwing knives
	- Master gives an enchanted ring.

- If you opened the missive then the Siblings will ambush you in your sleep at a random time later, but only once you complete or permanently fail the monastery quests
